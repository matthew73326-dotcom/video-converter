Using it in Roblox Studio

  1. Convert your video and download VideoData.rbxm.
  2. In Studio, find your VideoPlayer script and delete the old VideoData inside it.
  3. Right-click VideoPlayer → Insert from File… → pick VideoData.rbxm.
  4. Press Play.

The video is split into lots of small Part modules inside VideoData so Studio doesn't choke on one huge script. Every player downloads the whole file when they join, so try to keep it under about 20 MB. Lower the resolution or length, or raise the flicker filter, to shrink it. For a cylinder, use a wide shape (e.g. 192 × 64) so it isn't stretched.
